# FIZZL Digital Twin — Step 50

## Role Fit Engine

Adds a recruiter-facing, deterministic role-fit view for four documented role families:
- Junior AI / AI Implementation
- AI Customer Service / Automation
- Customer Success / Account Management
- Sales / Commercial

The engine uses the existing Knowledge Base and does not call the LLM. Each result separates documented strengths, documented gaps/limits and a practical next step.

This is intentionally evidence-based: it does not infer a formal qualification, seniority or production experience that is not documented.
