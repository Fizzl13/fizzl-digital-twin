# STEP 32 — Scenario Engine v1

FIZZL Digital Twin can now recognize practical/hypothetical business scenarios and apply Frits's documented decision principles as a grounded action framework.

## Added
- `server/scenario-engine.js`
- scenario classification for customer issues, commercial opportunities, automation, prioritization and general problem solving
- scenario framework injected into the AI prompt
- no hidden chain-of-thought exposure
- no invented thresholds, policies or facts
- showcase metadata updated with Scenario Engine

## Example
"Hoe zou Frits een inefficiënt klantenserviceproces met AI aanpakken?"

The system can answer using:
1. understand/validate the problem
2. use relevant data
3. determine whether AI is appropriate
4. keep human control where needed
5. improve from human feedback
