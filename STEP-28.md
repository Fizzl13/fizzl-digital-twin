# FIZZL DIGITAL TWIN — STEP 28

## Conversational AI Layer

Improves the Digital Twin's response quality so it behaves more like a polished professional portfolio assistant while remaining strictly grounded in the Knowledge Base.

### Improvements
- Natural, recruiter-friendly response style.
- Dutch/English language matching.
- Better follow-up conversation behavior using server-side session history.
- Concise answers for factual questions and structured answers for broader questions.
- Achievement questions lead with the relevant documented result or metric.
- Work-experience questions present employer, role and period directly.
- Unknown information is handled honestly without unnecessary meta commentary.
- No hallucinated facts, qualifications, projects, dates or metrics.
